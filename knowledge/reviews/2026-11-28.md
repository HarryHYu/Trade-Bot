## Patterns (hypotheses, n=10)
1. **We under-use dated catalysts with visible evidence** (TTWO, CAPR, BRBR, ABY): the information was public and dated; we
   stayed neutral or waited for the wrong date. Fix: binary-event and dated-catalyst rules added to Opus's research checks;
   watch re-checks (brain.recheck_watch) already in place.
2. **Public-data drivers get priced before the announcement** (TBLD): confirmation becomes sell-the-news. Fix: "noticed"
   check now compares the move since the data first showed and broker targets.
3. **Our right directions were often right for other reasons** (BRBR, CVI, AMD): the real drivers were company actions
   (self-help plans, deals, short reports). These go to the movers bank so the swarm hunts their early signs.
4. **Avoid/wait calls were our best** (IREN, AMD, DINO watch): judgement on what not to buy is sound; conversion of good
   short ideas into trades is the gap (DINO/CVI never traded).

All ten are filed in knowledge/movers/ with early signs and "look for next time" rules.
