## CVI from 2026-10-27: -47% (+2.7 sigma vs its normal moves)
- What actually moved it: Nov 3: record Q3 (adj. EPS $4.00 vs ~$2.5-2.9) plus a $4.2B purchase of Marathon's St. Paul Park and Mandan refineries funded by a $2.5B secured bridge, $500M from Icahn Enterprises, $800M from Trafigura and a $750M share offering ($250M secondary by an IEP unit); dividend held at $0.10. -13% that day, $38 by Nov 6; then the refiner selloff (diesel crack $102 -> $83, Brent -18.5%) took it to ~$30.
- Early signs (observable beforehand): Cracks rolling over from mid-October and Russian diesel export quotas (Reuters ~Oct 27) were visible; the deal itself was not signalled, but Icahn's history of using CVI for deals plus a windfall cash pile were standing risks.
- Look for next time: Controlled refiners (Icahn/IEP) sitting on a windfall quarter: price in a levered, dilutive deal or the controlling holder selling into strength on the print date, on top of cracks normalising.
- Our call: watch_short (manual): Refiners price peak cracks as durable while diesel cracks fall. Verdict lucky, signal played_out, moved by other_news.
