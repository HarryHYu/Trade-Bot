# RBLX Q3 2026 earnings prediction (report 2026-10-29 after US close = Oct 30 morning Sydney). Frozen 2026-10-27.

## Owner's hypothesis (to test)
AI lowered the cost of making games, so players now cycle through many short-lived "slop" games. Either
(a) more is spent because each new game has new things to buy, or (b) less, because players won't invest
in games they expect to drop. Analysts fixate on the one big hit (Steal an Egg) after missing Grow a Garden.

## Evidence gathered (primary or near-primary)
- Roblox explore API 'top-earning' sort = ranked by Robux spent in last 7 days (snapshot 2026-10-27 ~10:15 UTC):
  1 RIVALS (135k CCU, #7 by players), 2 Murder Mystery 2 (449k, #2), 3 Steal An Egg (801k, #1 by players),
  4 Adopt Me, 5 Blox Fruits, ... 10 Bloodlines (8.5k CCU). Grow a Garden now #57 earning, ~13.7k CCU.
- Games <120 days old: 17 of top-50 by players, 11 of top-50 by spend, mostly ranked 20-50 by spend.
- Concentration of the top-100 now: #1 = 15.7%, top-10 = 51.4%; 9 games >100k CCU. A year ago single games hit 20M+.
- CFO, Q2 call (Jul 30): engagement moved from high-monetizing 2025 viral games to "new and evergreen experiences
  with lower hourly monetization"; discovery now optimizes retention "at the expense of near-term monetization";
  under-13 bookings/hour below forecast. Q3 guide assumes the softness persists.
- Guide (Jul 30): bookings $1,576-1,653M (-14% to -18% YoY vs $1,921.8M), revenue $1,413-1,490M. Consensus bookings
  ~$1.63B, DAU 130.65M (Q2 123M, Q3'25 151.5M), hours 32.24B (Q2 29B).
- Steal An Egg created 2026-07-25 (after the guide was built), peak CCU 12.4M (rblxstat, Oct 3) to 14.2M (Aream/RoVitals).
- Mobile IAP, AppMagic net (2026, same methodology): Apr $94.4M, May $97.3M, Jun n/a (~$97M assumed),
  Jul ~$98-102M, Aug $100.6M, Sep $98M. Q3 vs Q2 about +3.5%. 2025 AppMagic data unreliable (iOS tracking gap
  from Mar 2025), so YoY from trackers is not used.
- Sensor Tower (Gamigion): Sep 2026 IAP $160M, "highest September on record", ~2x MoM, driven by the 20th
  anniversary "The Hunt" event (Sep 16-28, $54M net in the event window); record 532M MAU.
- Mobile downloads (AppMagic): Q3 2026 ~65.1M vs Q3 2025 ~98.5M (-34%). New-user funnel much weaker YoY.
- Roblox Plus replaced Premium Apr 30, 2026 ($4.99/mo). Web/gift-card purchase bonus reportedly removed for
  Premium holders from May 30 (single source): could shift purchases to mobile and flatter mobile trackers.

## Verdict on the hypothesis
(a) is NOT supported: new, fast-cycling games under-index on spend; the biggest hit ranks only #3 by Robux.
(b) IS supported, and it is the CFO's own explanation. It is not hidden: it is in the guide. What the market may
underweight is that it is structural (algorithm favours retention, AI keeps supply of cheap games rising), so
bookings per hour keep falling even as engagement recovers.
