## 2026-10-30: Q3 2026 hindsight (stock ~+30% from Sep 29 low into the print, then ~-17% after hours)
What was knowable before the print and we did not use:
1. Reaction history: the last prints moved on the NEXT-quarter guide, not the quarter: Feb 2026 +10%, Apr 30 -18%, Jul 30 -27%.
   Three of the last four were big drops on guidance. Base rate alone said: do not hold a +30% run through this print.
2. Next-quarter consensus: Street had Q4 bookings at ~$1.99-2.01B (about -10% YoY). Our own Q4 estimate mid was ~$1.975B,
   i.e. we already leaned BELOW consensus and never compared the two.
3. GTA VI launches Nov 19, 2026 (inside Q4). Management named it as a Q4 headwind. A huge competing launch for the same
   teen time and wallets was on the public calendar for a year; we never looked at competitor launch dates.
4. Our own October data pointed down for Q4: Steal an Egg 30-day average -26% vs the prior 30 days; mobile downloads
   -34% YoY in Q3. We used tracker data only for the reported quarter, not for the guided one.
5. Expectations ran up with the stock: a DAU estimate of ~145.8M was reported after the print, versus the ~130.7M we
   recorded earlier (unconfirmed which is right). Rallies raise the bar; re-check consensus in the last days.
6. Print-day volume ~3x normal (21.2M vs ~7M) with the stock down before the release. A short report was reportedly
   published Oct 27 (single source, unconfirmed).
Rule: for a run-up trade into earnings, exit by T-1 unless we have a quantified edge on the next-quarter guide vs consensus.
