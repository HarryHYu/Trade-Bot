# RBLX profile  (type: consumer-gaming)

## What the business sells and how it books revenue
Robux sold to players. Bookings = Robux sold in the quarter; revenue recognises bookings over the
estimated paying-user lifetime, so revenue lags bookings. Bookings is the number that moves the stock.

## The numbers that move this stock
Bookings growth, DAUs, hours engaged, bookings per DAU (ABPDAU), and next-quarter bookings guidance.

## Proxies observable before the report
Concurrent players per top game (rblxdb, rblxstat, Roblox games API), Robux pricing and top-seller
charts, app store ranks. Lag: same quarter. Engagement does not equal monetisation: viral hits that
skew young (brainrot / egg style games) lift DAU and hours more than bookings.

## Read-through map
Unity (U) for creator/engine trends; TTWO, EA for youth gaming spend; payment and app store fees.

## How it reacts to earnings
Large moves; options typically price 12 to 15%. Q2 2026: EPS beat but shares fell on bookings.
Guidance on next-quarter bookings has driven the reaction more than the quarter itself.
