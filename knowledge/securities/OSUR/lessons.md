# OSUR lessons

- [2026-10-27] Process miss: a paper trade was proposed before primary-source diligence. Later checks found the Ebola EUA is lab-only, the test is not WHO-listed (so UN buyers cannot procure it), Q2 profit was a non-cash release, and a second activist 13D existed. Each changed the thesis.
  why: the research ran from headline to trade and treated "has the only authorised test" as revenue without checking label scope and buyer channel.
  fix: mandatory diligence fields in research; any unverified item caps the tier at small.

## 2026-11-02: stopped out
- OSUR fell 12.3% on Nov 2 (3.94 -> 3.455), a steady slide all session after a flat open, below the Oct 16 low (3.61). No 8-K, no news found.
  Ebola peers rose (APT +2.2%, LAKE +1.8%, QDEL +5.9%), IWM flat, XBI -2.5%: stock-specific selling.
- Only new filing: Vanguard 13G/A (Oct 30) at 4.82% (3.33M shares), i.e. trimmed under 5%. Possibly a holder exiting (inference, unconfirmed).
- Both stops hit (3.85 close rule, and the original 3.50 paper stop). Paper trade closed ~-17% from 4.17 entry, at small size.
- Lesson: the Ebola leg never got a confirming order or WHO listing in 4 weeks; the Oct 23 breakout was a sympathy pop. A catalyst that
  needs confirmation should carry a time stop: no confirmation within ~2 weeks -> exit, regardless of price.
