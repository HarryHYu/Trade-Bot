# Preliminary judgement of the desk's ideas, 2026-10-27 to 2026-11-27 (from ledger/REPORT.md)

Benchmark: SPY fell ~7% from Oct 27 (S&P closed more than 10% below its October high in late November).

## Headline
- Ideas we did NOT act on: 18/25 right vs benchmark, average edge +9.6%.
- Ideas we DID act on: 1/5 right, average edge -12.9%. Paper equity -4.2% on five trades.
- Our judgement about what to avoid was good; our choice of what to actually buy was bad.

## What worked (and we mostly didn't trade)
- Refiners short thesis (cracks falling, stocks pricing peak margins): all seven fell 12-47% (CVI -47%). Left on "watch".
- BRBR (whey costs easing): +73% after the Nov 17 report. Left on "watch".
- Oil after the midterms: right (-11% since); planned entry came after the move.
- Avoid calls: AI/miner rebound (IREN -51%, CIFR -32%), AMD after its +21% week (-31%), Myer at 0.25 (-10%).
- Earnings numbers: 6/6 times we leaned the right side of consensus; 5/8 ranges hit.

## What failed
- Every long we took in a falling market: OSUR (-17%), tinyBuild (stopped -13.5% despite a record-breaking game), W, RIG.
- Watch-longs: ABY (-41%), uranium (-23%), CRCL (-24%). Calibration: "S&P -10% by Feb" at 30% happened within two weeks.

## Lessons for the autonomous desk
1. Trade the bearish theses too: in this tape our short ideas were the best ones.
2. A watch with a dated trigger (BRBR's Nov 17 report) should become an armed order, not a note.
3. Measured drivers still lose to market beta: size down or hedge longs when the index is breaking down.
4. Probabilities for macro tail events were too low; treat regime breaks as more likely while correlation resets.
