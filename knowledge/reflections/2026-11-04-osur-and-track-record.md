## Why OSUR failed (root causes, most important first)
1. The transmission link was broken before we entered, and the evidence was public. The money in an African outbreak flows through
   WHO/UNICEF/partners, which buy WHO-listed tests. WHO listed PCR tests for Bundibugyo (Altona Jul 2, Cepheid Sep 3, BioFire Oct 22); OraQuick
   2.0 has only a US FDA EUA limited to certified labs, and was not even reported among tests under WHO evaluation. We wrote this risk down and
   still tiered it "standard". A check that fails on the buyer channel must cap the tier at watch (the code rule exists; the manual call ignored it).
2. The driver was hypothetical, not measured. "Outbreak grows -> they might get orders" had no first-hand data showing any order. Compare
   tinyBuild: players are counted every day. Act-now trades need a driver we can measure now.
3. Wrong legs for the style. Two of three legs (cash floor, activist/takeover) are slow valuation arguments. A floor says where a stock might stop
   in months; it does not stop a 12% drop on a Monday. Slow legs are not catalysts.
4. Bought a sympathy pop. Entry came 2 sessions after a +8.7% news-sympathy day, near the 4.38 high. Outbreak stocks historically spike on
   headlines and give it back (2014 Ebola: protective-gear names spiked and faded).
5. Illiquid small cap in a risk-off tape. ~$240M cap, under $5M a day traded; one seller (Vanguard trimmed below 5%, filed Oct 30) can move it 12%
   on no news. October was a de-risking month for speculative names.
6. No time stop. Four weeks without confirmation should have ended it before price did.

## Rules going forward
- ACT-NOW TEST: the driver must be measurable in first-hand data today (players, prices, orders, filings, contract awards). If it is "might",
  the tier is watch until it is confirmed.
- BUYER CHANNEL FIRST: for health/government products, name the actual buyer and the listing rule they buy under, and check the product is on
  it. Not listed = watch.
- NO SYMPATHY CHASE: no entry within 3 sessions of a headline pop unless new confirming data arrives; prefer a pullback entry.
- SLOW LEGS DON'T COUNT: cash floors, activists and takeover hopes can support conviction but cannot be the reason to enter now.
- CONFIRMATION TIME STOP: a thesis waiting on confirmation exits after ~10 trading days without it.
- LIQUIDITY: under ~$5M daily value or a market cap under ~$300M -> half size and only with a measured driver.
